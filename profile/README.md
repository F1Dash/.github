# F1Dash

**Real-Time Formula 1 Live Timing Dashboard**

[f1dash.net](https://f1dash.net) | [Discord Community](https://discord.gg/f1dash) | [Support](mailto:support@f1dash.net)

---

## About

F1Dash is a comprehensive live timing and telemetry platform built for Formula 1 fans. Track every session in real-time with driver positions, sector times, tire strategies, and team radio communications — all in one place.

Whether you're following practice, qualifying, or race day, F1Dash delivers the data you need with sub-second updates directly from the track.

---

## Features

### Live Timing
Real-time driver positions, gaps to leader, intervals, sector times, and tire compound tracking.  Color-coded sectors show personal bests and fastest laps at a glance. 

### Interactive Track Maps
Watch drivers move around the circuit in real-time with 2D and 3D visualization modes. Includes DRS zones, sector boundaries, and current track conditions.

### Team Radio
Listen to driver-engineer communications as they happen. AI-powered transcription makes every message readable, even in noisy conditions.

### Telemetry Analysis
Dive deep into driver performance with interactive speed traces, throttle and brake inputs, gear changes, and RPM data. Compare two drivers side-by-side to see where time is gained or lost.

### Race Control
Live flags, safety car deployments, investigations, and penalty notifications. Never miss a key moment during a session. 

### Session Replay
Missed a session?  Replay any practice, qualifying, or race with full timing data, team radio, and race control messages.  Adjustable playback speed from 0. 5x to 16x.

### Weather Radar
Real-time precipitation radar and forecasts for the circuit. Know when rain is approaching before it hits the track.

### Championship Standings
Current driver and constructor standings with points, wins, and podiums. Historical data going back over 70 years.
